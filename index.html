How to play

Slot each event into the right spot in history.

  1. The timeline starts with two anchor events, years shown.
  2. You're dealt 5 mystery cards one at a time — title shows, year hidden (????).
  3. Tap a glowing + orb where it belongs (or drag the card there), then hit Confirm.
  4. Nail the spot and the year is revealed and locks in. Miss, and you lose a life — the card still snaps to its true position.
  5. Place all 5 before your 3 lives run out. One puzzle a day.
